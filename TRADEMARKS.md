# Branding And Official Releases

TechSologic Inc. stewards Federation Privacy Library and authorizes official
releases. The AGPL software license does not grant permission to misrepresent
a modified build, fork, or independent service as an official TechSologic
release or endorsed service.

This policy concerns attribution and provenance. It adds no restrictions to
the software rights granted by [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Truthful references, compatibility descriptions, criticism, and research are
welcome. Identify independent and modified distributions clearly, preserve
required notices, and avoid implying sponsorship or endorsement.

The TechSologic and UnoLock names and associated marks remain with their
respective owners. Federation Privacy Library is a separate project name;
nothing here asserts exclusive ownership of generic terms such as federation.

## Official Releases

An official release must be identified through TechSologic's designated project
channels with its source revision, validation, license notices, and artifact
provenance. An account name, package name, copied logo, or mirror alone does
not establish official release status.

Independent applications and forks may participate under the protocol and
software license without official-release status.

## Continuity And Contact

Official stewardship follows the documented
[succession process](GOVERNANCE.md#succession-after-permanent-cessation).
Trademark rights, repository access, and release credentials require their
own authorized arrangements; this document does not transfer them.

For branding enquiries, use the non-technical contact in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
