# Contributor License Agreement

## For Contributors

**Open your PR normally. You keep your copyright.** Before your first original
contribution is merged, accept this agreement once in a PR comment using the
permanent link supplied by a maintainer:

> I agree to the Contributor License Agreement at [agreement revision link]
> for this PR and my future contributions to Federation Privacy Library.

Post this from your own account. Future PRs use the same acceptance. No routine
forms, emails, addresses, or company countersignatures are required.

## For Maintainers

- Complete the terms review and establish acceptance records before first use.
  Do not present this draft as an already reviewed agreement.
- Reuse existing acceptance for this project. For new contributors, supply a
  permanent link to the reviewed terms and retain their account identity,
  acceptance comment, timestamp, agreement revision, and first covered PR.
- Before merge, check that each author's original contribution is covered.
  Clarify ownership only when needed; keep personal documents private. Record
  when a change has no new copyrightable material, and review imported
  third-party material under its own license.
- Keep the accepted version: changed terms need fresh agreement if required for
  future contributions. Silence or a preselected checkbox is not acceptance.

These checks can be handled manually; no CLA bot is required. Follow the
[acceptance-record procedure](docs/legal/cla-records.md) and
[repository setup checklist](docs/maintainers/repository-setup.md).
