# CLA Acceptance Records

Status: procedure to establish before the first external original contribution
is merged. No contributor acceptances or completed legal review are asserted by
this document.

## Before First Use

An authorized TechSologic maintainer must complete review of the
[agreement](../../../CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md), resolve its draft status,
preserve the reviewed revision, and choose a durable location for acceptance
records. That location must be accessible to the maintainers who verify merges
and transferable through the documented succession process.

No bot or external signing service is required. A preselected checkbox, commit
signature, repository access, or PR submission is not acceptance.

## Evidence To Retain

For each accepted agreement, retain:

- contributor's stable GitHub account identity and acceptance-comment permalink;
- explicit acceptance text and timestamp;
- immutable repository link to the exact reviewed agreement revision;
- first PR covered by that acceptance;
- maintainer verification of coverage and any later scope changes.

Request additional ownership evidence only when needed, and keep personal,
employment, and company documents private. Do not collect mailing addresses,
identity documents, or signatures routinely.

## Before Merge

1. Identify the authors of new original copyrightable material.
2. Reuse acceptance already recorded for this project and applicable terms.
3. For an uncovered author, provide the permanent reviewed agreement link and
   request acceptance from that person's own account.
4. Verify authorization and third-party notices as applicable.
5. Record coverage, or the reason no new acceptance is needed, with the PR review.

A contributor retains copyright. Acceptance grants the rights stated in the
agreement; it is not a copyright assignment. Terms accepted for UnoLock
Federated Node do not automatically cover this repository.

Preserve old agreement versions and acceptance evidence when terms change.
Require fresh agreement where the revised terms need it; do not backdate
acceptance or infer consent from silence.
